# Paula's Choice return-policy snapshot

- Source: https://www.paulaschoice.com/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-paulachoice/default/dwce09dc17/files/PC_INVOICE_BACK_US.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-07-18
- Window: full refund or in-house credit for merchandise returned within 60 days of purchase.
- Extended window: returns from day 61 through day 180 receive in-house credit.
- Deductions: shipping fees are not refunded.
- Kits: partial kits are refunded as store credit.
