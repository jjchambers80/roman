# Sephora return-policy snapshot

- Source: https://www.sephora.com/beauty/returns-exchanges
- Retrieved: 2026-07-18
- Window: new or gently used products generally receive a refund to the original payment method within 30 days.
- Shipping: Sephora.com returns are free; original shipping is not refunded unless Sephora made an error.
- Requirements: proof of purchase and validation; some exclusions apply.
- Limits: return activity is monitored and returns may be refused for misuse or abuse.
