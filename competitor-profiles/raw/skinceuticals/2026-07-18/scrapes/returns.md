# SkinCeuticals return-policy snapshot

- Source: https://www.skinceuticals.com/customer-service-shipping-and-returns.html
- Retrieved: 2026-07-18
- Window: direct-site purchases received within 30 days of purchase are eligible for a full product-price refund.
- Shipping: complimentary prepaid return label; original shipping is not described as part of the product refund.
- Scope: only SkinCeuticals.com purchases.
- Limits: returns may be rejected or charged when requirements are not met or activity indicates abuse.
