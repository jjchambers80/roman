# Maelove return-policy snapshot

- Source: https://maelove.com/pages/returns
- Retrieved: 2026-07-18
- Window: return requests accepted within 100 calendar days of delivery.
- Return deadline: product must be returned within 21 days after initiating the request.
- Shipping: free return shipping within the United States; original delivery charges are not refunded.
- Refund: original payment method, normally issued within 10 business days after receipt.
- Limits: purchase and return activity is monitored; purchases and refunds may be denied for abuse or unauthorized resale.
