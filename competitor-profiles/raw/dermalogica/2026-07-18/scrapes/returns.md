# Dermalogica return-policy snapshot

- Source: https://www.dermalogica.com/pages/return-policy
- Retrieved: 2026-07-18
- Window: direct-site returns accepted for any reason within 30 days of the order date.
- Condition: new or gently used products in original packaging; nearly empty, tampered, or usage-damaged products are excluded.
- Shipping: prepaid return shipping with no handling or restocking fee.
- Exclusions: partial kits, gift sets, free samples, and gifts with purchase.
