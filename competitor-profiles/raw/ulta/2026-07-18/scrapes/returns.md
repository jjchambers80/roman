# Ulta Beauty return-policy snapshot

- Source: https://www.ulta.com/guestservices/all
- Retrieved: 2026-07-18
- Window: most new or gently used products receive an original-payment refund within 30 days.
- Extended window: returns from day 31 through day 60 receive merchandise credit.
- Requirements: proof of purchase or system verification; all original components.
- Limits: ID/contact information may be required; excessive returns, reseller activity, and fraud may be refused.
