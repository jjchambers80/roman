# Caldera + Lab return-policy snapshot

- Source: https://calderalab.com/pages/faq-contact
- Retrieved: 2026-07-18
- Unopened orders: accepted within 30 days of delivery when the original shipping box remains sealed.
- Opened/tried products: covered by a 60-day satisfaction guarantee measured from purchase.
- Limits: no multiple refunds for the same product; refund activity is monitored; claims may be limited or refused for misuse or abuse.
- Customer contact: help@calderalab.com.
